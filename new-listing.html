Universal photo guidelines
  • Cover format: 3:4 portrait, recommended 1200 × 1600 px or higher.
  • Crosslisting compatibility: Center the merchandise within a square-safe zone for eBay and Poshmark.
  • Framing: Aim for the product to occupy approximately 80–90% of the usable frame without cropping important details.
  • Quality: Sharp, well-lit, accurate colors, neutral background.
  • Editing: Use SquareSeat or a similar editing app to crop, straighten, correct lighting, and adjust proportions.
  • Photo order: Main view → additional angles → details → labels → flaws → measurements/packaging.
  • Storage: Save original and edited photos in the item's OneDrive folder.
  • Upload destination: Upload final listing photos to Flyp.
